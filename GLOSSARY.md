# Knowledge Sharing Sessions

A collection of self-contained teaching materials for knowledge sharing sessions, each one usable both by the presenter and by attendees after the session.

## Language

**Topic**:
A single subject being taught, together with everything needed to present it (demo code, slides, presenter notes). One Topic can be presented many times.
_Avoid_: Project, demo, talk, presentation

**Session**:
One delivery of a Topic to a specific audience on a specific date.
_Avoid_: KSS, meeting, workshop, talk

**Script**:
The presenter's full run-through of a Topic, including the live demo parts that happen outside the slides. Written in the language the Session is held in.
_Avoid_: Scenario, transcript

**Speaker notes**:
Short per-slide cues for the presenter, embedded in the slides. They complement the Script and do not replace it.
_Avoid_: Notes, script
